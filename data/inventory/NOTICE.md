## Legal Boundary & Collective Database Note

Per OpenStreetMap Foundation Community Guidelines on Collective Databases, FloodRoute keeps external proprietary risk attributes, model coefficients, predictions, and sensor observations in separate tables keyed by segment/way IDs to maintain clear boundaries between the OpenStreetMap-derived geographic geometries and FloodRoute's proprietary scoring layers.
