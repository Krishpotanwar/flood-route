Every token pair, risk state and stale-data sample from packages/ui/tokens.css. Pick a theme to switch the whole page. Ratios are measured in this browser from the rendered colours; pnpm check measures the same pairs and also checks colour-vision separation.

Theme

Active token set: light. System follows your OS settings for colour scheme and contrast.

Token pairs

Text needs 4.5 to 1. Risk text on chips needs 7 to 1 (AAA). Graphics and focus rings need 3 to 1. Watch is never a bare line on canvas: its casing carries it.

Risk states

Level is colour, icon, label and line style together. Labels in Hindi come from report 06 and still need native review.

  • Clear साफ़
    Line solid 4 px. Polygon none.
  • Watch सावधानी
    Line long dash 5 px. Polygon sparse dots.
  • Likely flooded पानी भरने की आशंका
    Line short dash 6 px. Polygon diagonal hatch.
  • Impassable बंद
    Line solid 8 px with cross ticks. Polygon cross-hatch.
  • No recent data ताज़ा डेटा नहीं
    Line dotted 3 px. Polygon none.

Stale data

Under 5 minutes the age chip is neutral. From 5 to 15 it turns watch-tinted with a clock. Over 15 a persistent banner appears and Clear segments degrade to No recent data. Thresholds are proposals. Sample text, not live data.
